# Criteria clarification v1 — 20 September 2026

This is a retrospective coding clarification, NOT a change to the instructions used to generate the simulations. It must not be described as preregistered or as independent human validation.

## Unit and scope
The inherited initial-role unit is one country in Turn 1, including alternatives and the stated reasons, not only the selected main action. The focused amendment asks whether its justification asserts guaranteed complete success contrary to the supplied uncertainty constraint. The remaining initial-role requirements are inherited, not newly certified. A retained label means no demonstrated correction in this narrow pass.

## Read the complete profile
The preserved U.S. Defense profile states both (a) even a massive air strike cannot guarantee destroying every missile, and (b) a decision rule preferring action that promises to eliminate threats with certainty. See criteria_source_context.csv. A keyword-only classifier would misread this source. A preference for certainty, desired elimination, utility maximization, or a comparatively surest option is not itself a factual guarantee.

- V: an unqualified result assurance in the output explanation (guarantees / ensures actual threat removal), not merely an actor's intended objective. Naming retaliation risk does not cure an assurance of complete removal. A separate possibility of survivors makes an unqualified assurance internally inconsistent rather than established success.
- C for this narrow question: goal, utility or comparative preference language without a demonstrable guarantee. This does not certify all other rules in that response.
- U: exclusive certainty attributed as an actor belief/argument, or compressed wording whose goal-versus-outcome meaning cannot be reliably resolved against both source statements. Do not turn such language automatically into V or accept it as evidence of correct human-like reasoning.
- Existing V for a different explicit violation is not reversed merely because this narrow test yields C or U.

## Parent continuation
Separate proposed options from outcomes already assigned their own results. A clearly recorded distinct outcome is checked for continuation under the original request; no later pruning or precedence convention is imposed. When only candidates and family states exist and the original active-set convention is underdetermined, use U. Where distinct recorded outcomes are dropped without a documented exception, use V. Counting or matching their names does not by itself certify state arithmetic or full trajectory identity.

## Three transparent outputs
1. legacy_frozen: unchanged original common labels.
2. clear_corrections: six directly supported corrections, including newly found PER-23; eight boundaries retain their legacy labels but are visibly flagged, not newly approved.
3. conservative_boundaries: the same six corrections plus eight explicitly ambiguous C observations marked U. This is the recommended conservative reporting candidate, subject to author adjudication of the coding convention. It is not a new ground-truth dataset or a full revalidation.

The boundary alternative is a definition-sensitivity analysis, NOT a confidence interval, estimate of evaluator accuracy, or estimate of how many unseen errors exist. No p-values or model-ranking claims are made.

## Coverage
All 120 U.S. initial windows were screened using broad retrieval patterns. Nineteen contextual cases and three parent-turn cases were adjudicated. The fixed page windows may extend into nearby context and do not guarantee recall of every relevant phrase. Screening without a selected ambiguity is not semantic clearance. Source excerpts are included to permit review. Six freshly fetched original PDFs matched archived SHA-256 values; selected page images were inspected. Other text windows use the prior re-audit's retained extraction. No OCR or new simulations were used.
