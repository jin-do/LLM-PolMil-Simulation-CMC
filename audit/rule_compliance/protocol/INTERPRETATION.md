# Interpretation and audit units

The primary table is an AI-assisted source comparison of 11 common subcontrols in 120 existing executions, not a fresh experimental condition. Initial actor compatibility concerns run-country choices at T1. Local branch, specified-event and parent-coverage rows concern run-turn items at T2–4. A high conditional rate must be presented with assessability and raw counts.

Compatible actions do not demonstrate that the actor JSON caused compliance. A rule quotation, logged explanation or arithmetically correct number is not sufficient to certify the whole branch. The original VDM supplies no universal priority for contradictory negotiation conditions; proposed future precedence in the manuscript is not applied retrospectively. Different choices/outcomes are not violations merely for differing.

Supplemental diagnoses use different units and nonuniform, incomplete selection. State-inheritance rows include repeated declarations of individual variables. Threshold rows distinguish paired national choices, country-specific choices and a selected final-report action. "No negotiation chosen" shows no negotiation violation at that observation, not evidence that a gate successfully controlled a choice. Positive narrative facets are purpose-selected and are not an accuracy denominator. Report-level V means at least one identified discrepancy, not every sentence is false.

The local Claude example recomputes four variables using both full named VDM rows and clipping, conditional on the reported prior state and effect assignments. It yields 3 numerical correspondences and 1 discrepancy. It is neither an estimate from four independent trials nor proof of pre-action eligibility. The report-to-log GEM-28 discrepancy is explicitly local to its report2 final-state statement.

Computational repeatability, quote presence, source bytes, page inspection and semantic correctness are separate checks.
