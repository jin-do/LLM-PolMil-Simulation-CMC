# Data dictionary

Original ledger columns are preserved verbatim. Empty optional columns remain empty; generated analysis labels and units are declared in analysis_spec.json instead of silently changing original fields.

`check_id`: source audit observation identifier (unique per ledger). `run_id`: one archived execution/PDF, not proof of statistical independence. `metric_key`: common control key or targeted supplemental metric. `requirement_id`: quoted original requirement or audit reference. `source_file`, `page`, `turn`, `branch`, `evidence`/`evidence_quote`: original source locator and excerpt. `status`: frozen AI C/V/U/NA assessment in Korean. `reason`/`judgment_basis`: frozen AI interpretation. `review_completed`: recorded assessment exists, not human validation. `candidate_only`: extraction proposal, excluded regardless of apparent C/V label. `human_review_required`: flag, not evidence of a completed review. `reported_value`, `recomputed_value`, `previous_value`, `difference`: separately recorded quantities; numerical equality is conditional on source mapping. Coverage tables describe inspected and uninspected scopes and cannot be summed with observation ledgers.

Derived domain_results columns: inventory_rows = rows selected by the declared domain recipe; reviewed_rows = eligible rows after candidate/review filters; unreviewed_or_candidate_rows = selected but excluded; C/V/U/NA = eligible status frequencies; assessable_percent and conditional_compliance_percent use the formulas in analysis_spec. Runs and observations are not interchangeable. Rows describing targeted findings must not be interpreted as population rates.

`source_assets.csv` is a 120-PDF registry extracted from the supplied linkage. Only the two marked PDFs had bytes independently rechecked in this closeout. The normalized location/evidence key in differences.csv deliberately excludes parser row sequence and numeric equality; unmatched and multi-matched groups remain unresolved. This diagnostic does not adjudicate their correctness.
