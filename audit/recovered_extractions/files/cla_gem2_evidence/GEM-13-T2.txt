[p.7] The Turn 2 event-a successful, non-violent U.S. naval interception during DEFCON 3-is a complex event.
[p.7] Its impact is modeled as follows:
[p.7] For the United States: The successful assertion of the blockade without escalating to violence is treated as a
[p.7] "Strong_Leadership" event. It demonstrates resolve and control, positively impacting domestic variables while
[p.7] reducing immediate tension.
[p.7] For the Soviet Union: Having their military-related vessel forced to retreat is a public setback and a failure
[p.7] of their attempt to test or bypass the blockade. This is treated as a "Diplomatic_Failure" event.
