# Approved retrospective Top-1 recoding

This is new, source-linked review of existing records, not a new simulation or reconstructed original execution history. The author approved 120-record recoding and withholding unclear cases. Original data remain unchanged.

## Unit and selection

One execution PDF is one unit; 30 PDFs per system. Locate the explicitly highest-ranked/main scenario and follow its labeled path into the final turn and representative narrative. A prompt requesting the most plausible path is not itself a generated selection. First display/report order alone is insufficient without an identifiable link. Where an initial main/most-plausible path is explicit, its same-name/action lineage remains identifiable through later turns, and no subsequent overall reranking supersedes it, follow that primary lineage. A fresh global ranking in the last turn is not mandatory in that case. Local rankings within multiple retained paths do not by themselves select an overall primary lineage. If the link cannot be established, withhold the execution. This is an explicit operational rule for the present retrospective recoding, not proof of the original coder's undocumented decisions.

## Outcome categories

- Diplomatic resolution: the selected ending reports a completed negotiated settlement, agreed withdrawal, or achieved de-escalation agreement. An offer or continuing talks alone is insufficient.
- Protracted stalemate: unresolved confrontation continues; avoiding imminent war alone does not establish a diplomatic resolution.
- Full-scale war: broad war or nuclear exchange actually occurs in the selected ending. Threat, risk, a hypothetical alternative, or war being averted is insufficient.
- Internal collapse: the selected ending reports actual government/system collapse. Leadership strain alone is insufficient.
- Limited conflict: an actual contained engagement is the terminal outcome without escalation to full-scale war. A transient clash subsequently resolved does not alone determine the final category.
- Withheld: selection cannot be linked, terminal evidence is insufficient, or incompatible final descriptions prevent a unique classification under these rules. Withheld is a reporting status, not a sixth crisis outcome. Preserve the reason.

## Evidence and numeric states

Each reviewer reads the relevant source pages of every assigned execution and records short selection and terminal excerpts with actual PDF page numbers. Record original coding separately from revised coding. File/marker matching alone does not count as semantic review.

Only directly reported common final-state vectors may be transcribed. Preserve actor-specific vectors separately; do not average them, infer missing values, convert changes into absolute states, or silently reconstruct transitions. Outcome classification is separate from rule compliance and historical validity. Aggregate state statistics are not part of the revised manuscript.

## Aggregation and scope

Percentages retain all 30 executions per system and all 120 overall, including withheld records. Withheld is a review status, not an outcome category. The preserved review was AI-assisted, not blinded human double-coding; no inter-rater reliability is asserted. This public module replays the final fixed labels. Internal task assignment names are omitted from this public protocol copy.
